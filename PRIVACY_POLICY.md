# Privacy Policy for Game Hub

**Effective Date:** September 22, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 22, 2026  

Welcome to **Game Hub**! Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how **Game Hub** handles your information and data when you use our mobile application.

---

### 1. Overview
**Game Hub** is a casual mobile gaming application offering a collection of mini-games (including Tic Tac Toe, Memory Match, Mental Math, and Super Platformer). Game Hub is built with privacy-first principles: we do not collect, store, or share any personal information from users. All gameplay data, high scores, and settings are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to any external server or third party.

---

### 2. Information Collection and Use
* **Personal Data:** We do not collect any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or physical address).
* **Device & Usage Data:** No personal usage data, device identifiers (IMEI/MAC), or biometric data are tracked or harvested.
* **Gameplay Data:** All your game progress, settings, and scores remain strictly offline and local to your device storage.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services
Game Hub does not sell, rent, or trade user data to any third-party advertisers or data brokers.
* **No Advertising Trackers:** We do not include third-party behavioral advertising networks or tracking SDKs.
* **Platform Services:** Any underlying platform libraries (such as Google Play Services or Firebase Core/Remote Config) are utilized solely for essential application stability, runtime integrity, or local feature flags, without tracking individual user identities.

---

### 4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies
Game Hub does not use cookies, web beacons, tracking pixels, or any device fingerprinting technologies to monitor user activities across apps or websites.

---

### 5. Data Sharing
Your gameplay is completely private:
* We do **not** transmit your data to any external server.
* We do **not** share your gameplay data, scores, or preferences with any third parties.

---

### 6. Children’s Privacy
Game Hub is designed to be family-friendly and suitable for players of all ages, including children. Because we do not collect any personal information whatsoever, we do not knowingly solicit or collect data from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction) in accordance with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR-K regulations.

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage users to periodically review this page for any updates.

---

### 8. Contact Us
If you have any questions, suggestions, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or Game Hub, please contact us via our developer contact page or email.
